Your functions can evolve automatically. The DNA engine analyzes execution patterns, proposes AI-generated mutations, and deploys improvements via canary releases.

| Feature | Free | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
|---------|------|---------|--------------|------------|
| **DNA Profile & Insights** | Basic | Full | Full + predictive | Full + custom rules |
| **Auto-Evolution** | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Mutation Proposals** | — | Up to 5/day | Up to 10/day | Unlimited |
| **Canary Deployments** | — | 10% default | Custom % | Custom % + auto-rollback |
| **Mutation Cost** | — | 50 credits each | 50 credits each | 50 credits each |
| **Accept/Reject Variants** | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |

**How it works:**
1. The DNA engine monitors your function's execution
2. AI proposes code mutations (optimizations, bug fixes, features)
3. You review diffs and accept or reject each variant
4. Accepted mutations deploy via canary with automatic rollback on errors

## Common Questions

**Do all paid plans have a free trial?**  
Yes. Every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

**Can I change plans later?**  
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle.

**What happens if I exceed my limits?**  
We notify you before you hit limits. Free plans have a hard stop. Paid plans include generous allowances with overage billing—you only pay for what you use beyond your quota.

**Is there a pay-as-you-go option?**  
Yes. You can use the Pay-as-you-go tier with no monthly commitment. You pay a slightly higher per-request rate but only for what you use.

**Do you offer annual discounts?**  
Yes. Starter and Professional plans save 20% when billed annually. Enterprise plans save 30% and include volume-based pricing.

**What is Founder Mode?**  
Founder Mode lets you use Backend-in-a-Box bundles free for 3 months (or until you hit 100 users / $1K MRR). It is designed to help early-stage startups launch with zero upfront cost.

**How do DNA mutation credits work?**  
Each accepted mutation costs 50 credits from your wallet. Rejected mutations are free. You can buy credits starting at $1 for 100 credits.

**Are certifications free?**  
The Associate certification is free. Professional and Architect tiers have exam fees ($49 and $99).
