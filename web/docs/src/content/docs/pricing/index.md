## Common Questions

**Do you offer a free trial?**  
Yes. All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required.

**Can I change plans?**  
Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades at the end of your billing cycle.

**Is there a pay-as-you-go option?**  
Yes. Pay only for what you use with no monthly commitment.

**Need enterprise pricing?**  
[Contact our sales team](https://functionfly.com/contact) for custom SLAs, volume discounts, and on-premise deployment.
