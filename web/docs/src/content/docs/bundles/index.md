## Why Bundles?

Building a SaaS, marketplace, or AI app from scratch means stitching together dozens of services—auth, payments, databases, email, analytics. Bundles give you a head start:

- **One-click deployment** — Your backend is pre-configured and ready in seconds
- **Proven architecture** — Built on battle-tested patterns used by thousands of applications
- **Zero lock-in** — Each component can be customized or replaced
- **Automatic updates** — Security patches and feature updates applied seamlessly

## Getting Started

1. Choose a bundle that matches your use case
2. Click **Deploy** in the dashboard
3. Configure your domain and branding
4. Start building your frontend—your backend is ready
